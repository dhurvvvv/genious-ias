Branch Introduction

Serious Preparation For Serious Aspirants

The Genious IAS wing is dedicated entirely to civil services and government-job examinations — UPSC (IAS), HCS (Haryana Civil Services), State PCS, SSC, NDA, UGC-NET and more. Every programme is engineered around the exact demands of these examinations: conceptual depth, current affairs fluency, answer-writing skill and unshakeable exam temperament.

Led by Dr. Abhay Anand, the academy emphasises strong subject knowledge, continuous practice and doubt-clearing sessions — with personalised attention that has earned it a reputation as one of the region's most trusted institutes.

  • Structured curriculum: General Studies, Current Affairs & Optional Subjects
  • Both Preliminary and Main examination preparation
  • Flexible weekday, weekend and late-evening batches

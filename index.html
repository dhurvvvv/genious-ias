About Us

A Beacon of Excellence in Civil Services Preparation

Genious IAS is a premier coaching institute located in Rohtak, specialising in preparing students for the UPSC (Union Public Service Commission), HCS (Haryana Civil Services) and various other competitive examinations. With outstanding faculty and a proven track record, it has rapidly emerged as a beacon of excellence in the field.

Through the School of Humanities, the institute extends the same academic rigour to school students and law/management aspirants — making it a one-stop destination for academic and competitive exam preparation.

  • Comprehensive coaching for UPSC, HCS, SSC, NDA & more
  • UPSC Foundation programmes for Class 11th & 12th students
  • Personalised mentorship and dedicated doubt-clearing sessions

Our Second Branch

The School of Humanities — Where Futures Take Root Early

The School of Humanities nurtures students from Classes 11th & 12th through their board years while simultaneously preparing them for CLAT, IPMAT and CUET — with focused preparation, regular tests and personalised attention.

Our humanities programme builds strong foundations across Political Science, History, Sociology, Geography and Economics, guided by the most experienced and permanent faculty dedicated to competitive exam preparation.

  • Maximum selections in CLAT & IPM over recent years*
  • Integrated board + entrance exam strategy
  • Small-batch attention with continuous assessment

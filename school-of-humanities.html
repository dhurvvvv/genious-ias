Branch Introduction

Strong Foundations Today, Selections Tomorrow

The School of Humanities is the young scholars' wing of Genious IAS. It prepares Class 11th & 12th students across the full humanities spectrum — Political Science, History, Sociology, Geography and Economics — while simultaneously training them for CLAT, IPMAT and CUET.

The branch is recognised for producing the maximum selections in CLAT & IPM over recent years*, backed by the most experienced and permanent faculty dedicated to competitive exam preparation in Rohtak.

  • Focused preparation + regular tests + personalised attention
  • One roof for boards, law, management and university entrances
  • Early civil-services foundation for future aspirants

Claims marked * are drawn from the institute's published programme information.
